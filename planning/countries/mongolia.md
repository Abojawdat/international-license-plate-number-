# Mongolia — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Mongolia).

- 520×110mm. Format `1234 AAA`. Black on white.
- Since 2001: national "Soyombo" symbol in red required on the left, "MNG"
  oval between digit and letter groups.
- Mandated **Calibri typeface since 2018** — the only dossier so far
  specifying an off-the-shelf digital font rather than a custom/regulated
  face (UK's Charles Wright, HK/UK's BS AU145 specs) — potentially the
  simplest typography case found, worth remembering for an early build.
