# Tanzania — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Tanzania).

- 520×110mm, plus a double-line 340×180mm variant and a motorcycle
  half-size — another multi-size country.
- **Mainland uses `T` prefix, semi-autonomous Zanzibar uses `Z` prefix** —
  a within-country split tied to Tanzania's actual federal-ish structure
  (Zanzibar has its own government), different in kind from Egypt/Turkey's
  administrative-boundary format variance — this one's political, not just
  administrative.
- Private: white/yellow. Commercial: white/maroon.
- Security: embedded holograms, "TZ" watermark, embossed/tamper-resistant
  characters — similar security-feature depth to Armenia's Data Matrix
  approach, though visual/physical here rather than digital.
