# Bolivia — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bolivia).

- 300×152mm, current since 1999: `1234ABC`, 9 department letter codes.
  Blue/white private, yellow government, red public-service, red/white
  diplomatic, black/yellow international missions.
- Adopted the Mercosur `AB 12345` format Jan 2016 (associate-member
  alignment, not full-bloc like Brazil/Argentina/Uruguay/Paraguay) —
  worth checking whether Bolivia shares the literal blank or just the
  numbering convention.
