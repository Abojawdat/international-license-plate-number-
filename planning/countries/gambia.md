# Gambia — evidence dossier

**First pass, good history depth.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Gambia).

- Current (2019+): 520×110mm, black/white passenger, black/yellow
  commercial, FE-Schrift. Issued by **the Gambia Police Force directly**
  (`GPF` marking) rather than a transport ministry/DMV-equivalent — the
  issuing authority itself being a police force, not a civil agency, is a
  new case.
- 3-era region-code evolution: 1962-1999 short prefixes (GA, G1A...) →
  1999-2019 abbreviated divisions (WD, NBD, CRD) → 2019 expanded codes
  (WCR, NBR, CRR) — codes getting *longer* over time as administrative
  names got more precise, the opposite direction from most "codes get
  simplified over time" findings (Spain, Estonia, Slovakia).
