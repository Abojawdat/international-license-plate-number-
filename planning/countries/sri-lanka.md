# Sri Lanka — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Sri_Lanka), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/sri-lanka.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/sri-lanka.html).

- 520×110mm. Format: 2-3 English letters + 4 digits. Front black/white,
  rear black/yellow (same face-color-split pattern as UK/Algeria/Sri
  Lanka now 4 countries). Government: blue.
- **Province codes were removed from new registrations since Jan 2023** —
  a temporal change in the *opposite* direction from South Africa's
  (simplifying toward less regional info, not more uniformity for its own
  sake, though the direction of "recent regulatory change to track" is the
  same kind of finding). Older plates still show province code (e.g. WP)
  under the national emblem.
