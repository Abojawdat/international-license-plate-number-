# British Virgin Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_British_Virgin_Islands).

- Pre-1996: EU size (520×110). **1996: switched to NA size (300×152)** —
  a full dimension-standard switch, not just a format change, with a
  brief transitional `VI`-prefix system in 1995-96 bridging the two.
- Full category table: black/yellow private (`PV`), white/red government
  (`GV`), black/white commercial (`CM`) and taxi (`TX`/`TAXI`), black/green
  rental (`RT`).
