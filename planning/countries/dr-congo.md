# Democratic Republic of the Congo — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo).

- Current since April 2009: 480×112mm — close to but not identical to
  Iraq's 520×110 `modernLong`, another near-miss dimension case (same
  pattern as Jordan/DRC/New Zealand type discrepancies already logged).
- White/black. Format: 4 digits + 2 letters. Left: DRC flag + "CGO" text —
  Iraq-shaped `ColorBandStrip`.
- History: yellow-on-blue until 1979, then yellow-on-green until the 2009
  redesign — a real 3-era format history (current + 2 legacy), comparable
  in depth to Kuwait's multi-era finding.

Note: distinct from the Republic of the Congo (Congo-Brazzaville), a
separate country with its own dossier still needed.
