# Palau — evidence dossier

**First pass, the most architecturally significant Pacific dossier found.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Palau).

- **Federated down to the state level in a country of ~340 km²** — Palau's
  16 states each control their own plate design independently, the
  smallest-scale confirmed federation case (smaller than the Bahamas'
  per-island issuance).
- **Materials vary by state and include: stamped metal, hand-painted,
  stencilled, printed laminated paper on a wooden base, plexiglass, and
  plastic** — the widest material diversity confirmed in any single
  dossier; this alone means "the plate is always metal/reflective
  sheeting" can't be a baseline assumption once Pacific micro-states are
  in scope.
- National-level categories that *do* exist: government (orange/white,
  2019), judicial (silver/light-blue, 2020), diplomatic (light-blue/gold,
  ~2020) — recently introduced, dated precisely, and layered on top of
  otherwise state-controlled design.
- **A unified national plate was attempted in the 1990s (following 1980s
  political violence over state nuclear-policy conflicts) and was later
  abandoned, reverting to state-by-state production** — a third confirmed
  format oscillation (after Slovenia, Nunavut), and the only one tied
  directly to political conflict resolution rather than a design
  preference.
