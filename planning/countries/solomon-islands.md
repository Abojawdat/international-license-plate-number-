# Solomon Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Solomon_Islands) — a stub.

- 372×134mm (Australian standard; an older NA-standard spec is also
  mentioned, possibly a legacy size). Format `MA-0000` rolling through
  `MB-0000` etc.
- **Until 2014, motorists made their own plates; the government only took
  over official issuance that year** — the clearest *dated* transition
  from owner-manufactured to government-issued found, useful as a
  companion data point to Vanuatu/Tonga/Kiribati's still-current
  owner-manufactured rule.
- "The Hapi Islands" slogan.
