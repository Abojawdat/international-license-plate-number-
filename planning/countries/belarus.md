# Belarus — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Belarus).

- 520×110mm. Format varies by vehicle type structurally, not just by
  color: `1234 AB-5` cars, `AB 1234-5` heavy vehicles, `A1234B-5`
  trailers — the *character order itself* changes by category, not just
  a prefix/suffix/color.
- **EVs get an `E` prefix whose position differs between passenger cars
  and trucks/buses** — another distinct EV-encoding variant (6th+ found).
- Single-digit region codes (1-7, alphabetical by region name, 8 reserved)
  — the simplest region-coding scheme found (vs. Iraq's 2-digit or
  Turkey's 2-digit-of-81).
- 1996: "BELARUS" text replaced the national crest — a text-for-emblem
  swap, a new kind of design change.
