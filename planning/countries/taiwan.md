# Taiwan — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Taiwan).

- 320×150mm (cars), 220×140mm (motorcycles).
- Format extended to 7 characters (`AAA-0000`) since 2012. **Excludes I, O,
  and the digit 4** — the first exclusion found that isn't purely
  visual-confusion-based: 4 is excluded for cultural/superstition reasons
  (sounds like "death" in Chinese), not because it looks like another
  character. A new *category* of exclusion reason for the validator model,
  not just another instance of the same one.
- Colors: white/yellow = private, black = commercial/government, green =
  freight trucks and sub-50cc motorcycles.
