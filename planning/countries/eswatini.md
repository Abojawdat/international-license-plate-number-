# Eswatini — evidence dossier

**First pass, one of the most novel dossiers found.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Eswatini).

- **Background is a full pictorial scene — hills and traditional Swazi
  beehive huts over a sunset** — the only fully illustrated/landscape
  background found in any dossier; every other country's field is a flat
  or gradient color. This alone would need a wholly different rendering
  approach from `_PlatePainter`'s current flat-fill model if built.
- Format `JSD 136 AM` — random letter + "SD" + digits + random letter +
  regional letter, an unusually long combinatorial format explicitly
  sized for capacity (source states the exact combinatorial count:
  ~17.6 million).
- **2010 redesign was explicitly motivated by reducing hijacking risk for
  Swazi-registered cars in South Africa** — a crime-prevention-driven
  design change, a wholly new "why did the design change" reason, and
  reportedly controversial precisely because of that motive.
- 5-year validity with an expiry sticker; a windshield version encodes the
  plate number in a **QR code** — digital redundancy alongside the
  physical plate.
- Pre-1980: royal vehicles used `S` prefix, government `GSD` — a monarchy-
  specific category (Eswatini is a kingdom), joining Brunei/Bhutan/
  Luxembourg.
