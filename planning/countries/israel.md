# Israel — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Israel), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/israel.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/israel.html).

- 520×110mm. Format `123-45-678`. Black on yellow, both faces (no
  front/rear split like UK/Algeria/Tunisia).
- Left-side euroband: Israeli flag, `IL`, ישראל (Hebrew), إسرائيل (Arabic) —
  dual-script on the band itself, not the registration.
- Directly politically entangled with Palestine's plates (see `palestine.md`)
  — yellow-vs-green/white is reportedly used for movement restrictions at
  checkpoints. Flag for a sensitivity conversation alongside the other
  disputed-territory flags already in `_tracker.md`, independent of the
  data work itself.
