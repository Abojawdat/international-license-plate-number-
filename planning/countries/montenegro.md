# Montenegro — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Montenegro).

- 520×110mm, blue "MNE" strip + 2-letter municipal code + coat of arms +
  `AA 123` — municipal-level (not just national) region coding, a finer
  grain like most of ex-Yugoslavia's plate schemes.
- Categories: blue text police, green text military, white/light-green
  agricultural, yellow-on-white diplomatic (black/white for press/economic
  attachés — a diplomatic sub-tier distinction), brown/black oldtimers,
  `RP`-suffixed temporary, `TX`-prefixed mandatory taxi marking.
- **`CG`/`MN` letter combinations reserved exclusively for government,
  with `PG CG001` traditionally the President's official car** — another
  head-of-state reserved-plate case, joining the growing list.
- Former Yugoslav format phased out June 6, 2008 for the current EU-
  compliant design.
