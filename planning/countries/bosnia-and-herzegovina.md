# Bosnia and Herzegovina — evidence dossier

**First pass, one of the most significant findings in this whole tracker.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bosnia_and_Herzegovina).

- **The current uniform national format (since 1998) directly replaced
  entity-specific plates specifically because vehicles bearing one
  entity's plates were being vandalized when driven into the other
  entity** — the format exists to *remove ethnic/political identifiers*
  that were being used at checkpoints, a post-conflict freedom-of-movement
  measure. This is not a design preference or a capacity/political-treaty
  reason like North Macedonia's — it's a conflict-driven, physical-safety
  motivation. Read alongside Kosovo's 2022 plate-standardization protests
  and Cyprus's frozen shared-predecessor format, this makes **at least 3
  confirmed cases where license plate design is a genuine flashpoint for
  ethnic/political conflict**, not just an administrative detail. Worth a
  standalone note in `architecture-notes.md`: some countries in this
  tracker need political sensitivity handled as seriously as the data
  itself.
- 520×110mm, `A12-B-345`, black/white uniform across all of Bosnia.
- Categories: taxi (`TA-000000`), temporary (`TT-000000`, "Testne
  Tablice"), portable plates (2024+, `MT-000000`), oldtimer (2025+,
  `OT-000000` — very recently added), diplomatic (blue/yellow, limited
  letters).
