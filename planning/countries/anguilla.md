# Anguilla — evidence dossier

**First pass, one positional-migration finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Anguilla).

- 152×300mm, `A 1234`. Blue/white gradient with coat of arms, black
  letters. Category suffixes: `H` hire/taxi, `G` government, `R` rental.
- **The vehicle-type letter moved from a suffix to a prefix position** in
  the 2007 redesign (`P` now leads for personal vehicles) — a positional
  migration of the same category-letter concept, distinct from Guinea's
  letter-to-number *type* change; here the letter stays a letter but
  relocates within the serial.
- Governor's car: crown emblem on black — another British-overseas-
  territory Governor case (Cayman, TCI, Gibraltar).
- "Rainbow City" slogan since 2007. First plates 1950, white/black.
