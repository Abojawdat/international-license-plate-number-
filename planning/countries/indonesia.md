# Indonesia — evidence dossier

**Status: first pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Indonesia) plus a couple of Indonesian car-manufacturer blog explainers (Wuling, Chery Indonesia).

## What's known
- Dimensions: 460×135mm (cars), 275×115mm (motorcycles) — close to but not
  identical to the pattern-table's Indonesia entry (275×110 / 460×135),
  minor rounding difference only.
- Format: front letter (region) + digits (sequence) + rear letters
  (sub-region + vehicle type).
- Colors: white/black = private, yellow/black = commercial/public transport,
  red/white = government, green/black = free-trade-zone vehicles, black/
  white = diplomatic.
- **Electric vehicles get an added blue trim on the expiry-date row**,
  layered on top of whichever base category color already applies — a
  *different* mechanism from China's (separate green NEV plate replacing
  the color entirely) or India's (green replaces the category color). Three
  countries, three different ways of encoding "this is an EV" — genuinely
  unresolved design question for `architecture-notes.md`, not just a data
  gap.
