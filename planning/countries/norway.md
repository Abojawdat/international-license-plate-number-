# Norway — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Norway).

- 520×110mm, `AB 12345`. An unusually explicit letter-to-category mapping:
  A=standard, B=off-road/untaxed, C=rally/competition, D=military
  (orange), E=commercial (green), F=diplomatic (blue), G=dealer (red), H=
  temporary-test (red) — category as a *leading-letter code* with its own
  dedicated color per letter, a cleaner version of Poland's category-
  replaces-region-code pattern.
- **13 distinct EV prefix letters** (EL, EK, EV, EB, EC, ED, EE, EF, EH,
  EJ, EN, EP, ER) — the most elaborate EV-encoding mechanism found so far,
  a whole prefix-letter namespace rather than a color/trim/separate-plate.
- Material history: aluminum → plastic (2009-2011) → back to aluminum
  (2012) due to winter weather damage — a materials-driven format change,
  a new "why" reason (environmental/durability, not political/capacity).
