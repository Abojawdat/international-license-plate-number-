# United Kingdom — evidence dossier

**Status: first pass, well-corroborated.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_United_Kingdom) plus multiple UK plate-retailer/legal sites (garage.co.uk, regplates.com, regtransfers.co.uk) independently agreeing — a simpler, better-documented case than most so far.

## What's known
- Dimensions: 520×111mm (essentially Iraq's `modernLong`).
- Format: `AA11 AAA` — 2 letters (region/office code) + 2 digits (age
  identifier, changes twice yearly) + 3 letters (random).
- **Front and rear plates are different colors on the same vehicle**: white
  front, yellow rear — not a category distinction like every other dossier
  so far, but a **fixed front/back rule applying to every vehicle equally**.
  New axis: some countries vary color by *face*, not just by category.
- Mandated typeface (Charles Wright) and exact reflective-material spec
  (BS AU 145e) — the most precisely regulated single detail found in any
  dossier so far.

## Open questions
- None major — this is one of the cleanest, most consistent dossiers so
  far and a good candidate for an early "real" implementation once photo
  confirmation is in reach, precisely because it's simple.
