# Honduras — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Honduras).

- 520×110mm. 8-category table: personal (green), commercial (yellow),
  government (blue), diplomatic/consular/international-mission (red-
  purple for missions), trailers, light trucks (red) — category encoded
  both by prefix letter *and* color together.
- "Cuidemos los Bosques" (Take care of the forests) + Honduras map
  background — an environmental slogan baked into the plate, a new kind
  of decorative-but-fixed text alongside Australia's state slogans.
- **Oct 2018 redesign added a QR code** — joins Armenia/Romania's digital
  anti-counterfeiting trend; FE-Schrift typeface (4th country with this
  face); explicitly Mercosur-influenced, matching Guatemala's cross-
  reference to Honduras.
