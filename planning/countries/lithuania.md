# Lithuania — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Lithuania).

- 520×110mm, `ABC 123`, black/white, EU stripe since 2004, **coat of arms
  added Oct 2023** — a very recent incremental design addition to an
  otherwise-stable format, distinct from a full redesign.
- Taxi: white, `T` + 5 digits (changed from yellow to white in April 2018
  specifically, alongside dropping the sticker space — 2 unrelated changes
  landing in one date). Diplomatic: white/green, no EU sidebar, distinct
  `01 3 123` numeric-only format. Military: `LK` + 3 digits + 1 letter,
  white/black. Vintage: `H` + 5 digits.
