# South Africa — evidence dossier

**Status: first pass, architecturally important.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_South_Africa) plus a couple of consumer sites.

## What's known
- **Federated by province, not unitary** — each of the 9 provinces has its
  own design/colors/alphanumeric pattern (GP = Gauteng, WC = Western Cape,
  etc.) — a fifth confirmed case of this pattern (after US, UAE, Australia
  explicitly, Mexico partially).
- Yellow reflective plates are the de facto private-vehicle standard;
  government vehicles are red-text-on-white regardless of province.
- **A national standardization is reportedly rolling out from 2026**,
  replacing provincial designs with one uniform yellow/black design for all
  newly-registered vehicles. This is the first *forward-looking* format
  change found (vs. Iraq's/others' historical legacy formats, which are all
  in the past) — the format-history model needs to handle "an announced
  future format," not just past ones.
