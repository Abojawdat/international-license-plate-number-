# Northern Ireland — evidence dossier

**First pass, real confirmed divergence from Great Britain, one political
finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Northern_Ireland).

- 520×111mm, `AAA 1234` since Jan 1966 (evolved from 1903-era 2-letter
  codes exhausted by 1957). Same white-front/yellow-rear convention as
  the rest of the UK.
- **Northern Ireland vehicles are explicitly barred from displaying the
  England/Scotland/Wales regional-flag Euro-bands** available elsewhere
  in the UK — a real, currently-enforced political restriction on plate
  customization, not just a design difference. Worth pairing with the
  Bosnia/Kosovo/Cyprus political-plate findings (#39) even though the
  mechanism here is a *restriction* rather than a *redesign*.
- **UK-wide country code changed from "GB" to "UK" on Sept 28, 2021** —
  confirms Gibraltar's Brexit-driven change (`gibraltar.md`) wasn't
  Gibraltar-specific; the whole UK identifier system changed post-Brexit.
