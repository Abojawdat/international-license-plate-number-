# San Marino — evidence dossier

**First pass, one asymmetric-redesign finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_San_Marino).

- Standard 390×120mm; **since 2023, a reduced-size front plate specifically
  (300×100mm) while the rear stays standard size** — a redesign that
  changed *only one face's dimensions*, not both, a new variant of the
  front/rear-differs pattern (Tunisia/Italy/Switzerland/Monaco all have
  differing face sizes, but as a stable design, not a recent one-sided
  resize).
- Blue-on-white (RAL 5017), 2023 also darkened the serial font from light
  to medium blue — a legibility-driven color-value tweak, smaller in scope
  than Cayman's full recall but the same underlying motivation.
- Categories: `CD` diplomatic, `CRS` Red Cross, `POLIZIA` police, `PROVA`
  test vehicles, `R` trailers, `E` export, and a **now-discontinued `VE`
  electric-vehicle prefix (2019-2023)** — an EV-encoding mechanism that
  was tried and then retired, the first confirmed case of an EV scheme
  itself being rolled back rather than just varying by country.
- Custom/vanity plates available since 2004.
