# Laos — evidence dossier

**First pass, one novel EV-encoding finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Laos).

- 520×110mm, `AA 1234` since 2001. **Prefix letter is drawn from Lao
  script and directly encodes vehicle category** (specific Lao characters
  for passenger cars, a different one for heavy trucks) — requires Lao
  script rendering, another script family; category-as-script-character
  again (South Korea/Laos both do this, independently).
- Standard: black on orange (another non-white default, joining Cameroon/
  Netherlands-yellow/Belgium-red-ink outliers).
- Diplomatic/UN use Lao-script abbreviations rather than Latin codes.
- **Since 2024: EVs get a green "EV" sticker at the top-left corner** — a
  5th distinct EV-encoding mechanism (after China's separate plate, India's
  color swap, Indonesia's trim, Norway's prefix-letter-set): an applied
  sticker, layered on top of any existing design rather than baked into
  it at manufacture time.
