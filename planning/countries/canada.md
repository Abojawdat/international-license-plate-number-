# Canada — evidence dossier

**First pass, architecturally important — confirms the federated pattern
even more granularly than expected.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Canada) plus several individual province Wikipedia articles (Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, BC, Nunavut).

- 300×150mm generally, but **format, colors, slogans, and even
  front-vs-rear mounting requirements vary by province/territory** — BC,
  Manitoba, Ontario require both plates; everywhere else, rear only. That
  last detail is new: not just design varying by subdivision (already
  established for US/UAE/Australia/South Africa), but a *physical mounting
  requirement* varying by subdivision too.
- Northwest Territories: **polar-bear-shaped plate**, not just a
  differently-colored rectangle — the most extreme physical-shape variant
  found in any dossier so far, well beyond Kuwait's square motorcycle plate.
- Quebec: blue/white standard, green/white for EVs (color-swap EV encoding,
  same mechanism as India).
- Confirms: Canada needs the same `FederatedCountry` treatment as the US,
  with its own 13-entry sub-tracker, not a single dossier.
