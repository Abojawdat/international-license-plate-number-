# Ivory Coast — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Ivory_Coast).

- Current since June 2023 (very recent): `AB 123 CD`, 520×111mm. White on
  blue main field; right-side identifier strip white-on-orange with country
  outline + "CI" — a two-tone-strip layout not seen elsewhere (strip color
  differs from field color, both non-default).
- Diplomatic: black on orange. Temporary: white on green. Military: white
  on black.
- 3 concurrent sizes (car/square/motorcycle) — another multi-size country.
