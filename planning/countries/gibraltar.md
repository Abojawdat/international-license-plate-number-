# Gibraltar — evidence dossier

**First pass, one Brexit-driven finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Gibraltar).

- 520×110mm front white/rear yellow (UK-heritage face-split, matches HK).
  `G` + 4 digits + series letter, 2001 redesign anticipating exhaustion
  of the 1912-2001 `G`+5-digit scheme.
- **Jan 2021: EU flag/stars removed post-Brexit**, replaced with a plain
  blue band showing just "GBZ" — a design change driven by leaving a
  supranational political body, a new variant of the "why redesigned"
  catalogue distinct from North Macedonia's treaty-driven *code* change
  (this one removes a supranational symbol rather than renaming anything).
- `G 1`: Chief Minister's car. Governor's car: silver crown emblem instead
  of a serial — a 3rd British-overseas-territory Governor case (Cayman,
  Turks and Caicos).
