# Ecuador — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage, one novel design-change reason.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Ecuador).

- 404×154mm (matches the pattern-table figure exactly). Full category
  table: private (white-silver), commercial (orange), government (gold),
  municipal (lime green), diplomatic (blue), international orgs (red),
  military (black/white), motorcycles (yellow) — category also partly
  encoded by a specific *second letter* of the serial, not just color.
- **June 2012: switched from full-color plates to color only on the upper
  border**, explicitly to improve automated camera/radar readability — a
  technology-driven design-change reason (optimizing for machine reading),
  distinct from every other "why did it change" reason found so far
  (capacity, politics, cross-border misreading, materials, unification).
