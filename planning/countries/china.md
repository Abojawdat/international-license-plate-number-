# China — evidence dossier

**Status: first pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_China) plus several explainer sites (thechairmansbao.com, wildthyme.ca) agreeing on the core scheme.

## What's known
- Format: `[Province Hanzi character][City Latin letter][5-character sequence]`,
  e.g. 京A·12345. **Requires real Chinese-character (Hanzi) rendering** —
  neither Iraq's vector `PlateTypeface` (Latin+digits) nor a simple
  Arabic-script fallback covers this. First confirmed case needing a third
  script family.
- Colors: blue = private car, yellow = large/commercial, **green = new
  energy vehicle (NEV)**, black = foreign-owned/SAR-registered, white+red
  text = government/police/military.
- **NEV plates are physically longer**: 7 characters vs. 6 on standard
  plates, not just a different color — propulsion type changing the format
  length itself, not just a color swap. Strongest version yet of the
  "propulsion type is an axis" finding (see India, Indonesia below, and the
  earlier pattern-table 440×140 vs 480×140 dimension note already in
  `_tracker.md`).
