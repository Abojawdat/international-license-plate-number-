# Bermuda — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Bermuda).

- Two concurrent sizes (EU 520×110 most plates, NA 152×300 for
  personalized only) — sizing tied to plate *purpose* (personalized vs.
  standard), not vehicle type like every other multi-size case so far.
- Black/white standard since 1975. Auxiliary cycles: white/blue. Rental
  auxiliary cycles: red/white.
- Classic-car category (`CL` prefix) explicitly **permits multiple vehicle
  ownership under one categorization** — a legal/business rule embedded in
  the category system, not just a design one, worth remembering the data
  model may need to carry non-visual category rules too.
- Personalized plates: up to 7 characters overlaid on a map of the island.
