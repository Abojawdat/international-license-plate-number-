# Slovenia — evidence dossier

**First pass, one oscillation finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Slovenia).

- 520×120mm (taller than the 110 default, joins Austria). 12 region codes
  (CE, GO, KK, KP, KR, LJ, MB, MS, NM, PO, SG + more), a shield sticker
  denoting city subdivision within a region — a 2-tier code plus a sticker
  layer, more granular than most.
- Colors: black/white standard, green diplomatic letters, blue/white
  police, black/yellow export, white/green agricultural.
- **2004: adopted EU blue-band format; by 2008, reverted to a green-border
  design while keeping the EU band** — a design change that partially
  *reversed itself* within 4 years, the first confirmed oscillation found
  (every other format-history case so far moves in one direction and
  stays).
