# Bahamas — evidence dossier

**First pass, two novel findings.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Bahamas).

- 152×300mm, `AA 1234`, Bahamian flag at bottom.
- **Plates are issued per-island/island-group**, not centrally — a
  federated pattern at a smaller scale than the US/Canada/UAE/Australia
  cases, worth noting the pattern isn't limited to large countries.
- Deep format/color history (6 eras 1977-present) including a **1992-1996
  special commemorative truck-plate design marking the 500th anniversary
  of Columbus's landfall** — the first confirmed *limited-run commemorative
  edition* found, a category of "why does a design exist temporarily" that
  isn't a permanent format at all.
