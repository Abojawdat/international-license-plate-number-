# Zimbabwe — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Zimbabwe).

- 520×110mm. Format `ABC 1234`, Zimbabwe Coat of Arms centered on plate —
  an emblem placed mid-plate rather than on a strip/band, a layout variant
  not seen elsewhere.
- Private: black/yellow (white background introduced 2006 as an option).
  Commercial: red/white.
- Rear-only for motorcycles/trailers (front+rear for everything else) —
  a per-vehicle-type mounting rule, not just a color/format rule.
