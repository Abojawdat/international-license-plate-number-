# Suriname — evidence dossier

**Second pass attempted, one significant finding, category data still
genuinely unavailable.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Suriname), plus the Dutch-language Wikipedia article (`nl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surinaams_kenteken`) which the first pass hadn't checked.

- 300×152mm. Black on yellow (default, not just a category — joins
  Netherlands/Cameroon/Laos as yellow-default outliers). Format: 2 letters
  (always starting `P`) + 4 digits.
- Mid-2025: blue plates introduced specifically for one government
  department's service vehicles (Openbaar Groen en Afvalbeheer, public
  greenery/waste management) — a category this narrow (one department, not
  "government" broadly) hasn't appeared in any other dossier.
- **Suriname has reportedly been examining a switch to a person-bound
  license plate** (`persoonsgebonden kenteken`) as of Aug 2021 — if
  adopted, this would be a third confirmed driver-bound case after Cuba
  and Belgium, and the first one caught *before* adoption rather than
  after. Worth revisiting this dossier specifically to check whether it
  went into effect.
- Full category-color table: still not found after two real passes across
  two language sources — treat as genuinely unavailable via search, not a
  gap to keep re-attempting the same way.
