# Guyana — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage, one head-of-state finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Guyana).

- White/black default (outlier, joins Malaysia's default). Category by
  leading letter: P=private, H=hire/taxi, B=bus, G=goods, C=cycle,
  T=trailer, DPL/DC=diplomatic (black/yellow), DFB=defence force,
  CRG/GUY=state pool vehicles.
- **President's vehicle: gold Cacique's Crown emblem; Prime Minister's:
  gold national coat-of-arms on black** — a head-of-state-specific plate,
  broadening the "royal/monarchy-specific category" finding (Brunei,
  Bhutan) to republics too: it's a *head-of-state* pattern, not strictly
  a monarchy one.
- Series progression is literally trackable (SS/2013 → XX/2018 → PAG by
  April 2024) — another live-exhaustion-tracking case (Fiji, Trinidad).
