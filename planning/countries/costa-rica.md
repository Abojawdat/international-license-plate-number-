# Costa Rica — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Costa_Rica), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/costa-rica.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/costa-rica.html).

- 520×110mm. Format `ABC-123`. Black on white. "COSTA RICA" top,
  "CENTRAL AMERICA" bottom — regional-bloc text alongside the country name,
  a detail also expected in Guatemala/Honduras (see below), suggesting a
  Central-American design convention worth checking as a group rather than
  purely per-country.
- Rolled out mid-2013 to early 2015; hologram + security strip.
