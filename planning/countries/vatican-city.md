# Vatican City — evidence dossier

**First pass, richly documented head-of-state case.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Vatican_City).

- 520×110mm, black/white, non-standard serial. `CV` (Città del Vaticano)
  private, `SCV` (Status Civitatis Vaticanae) government/official.
- **The Popemobile carries plate "SCV 1" in red lettering** — the most
  photographically-documented head-of-state plate found (specific
  citations from Pope John Paul II 2004 and Pope Benedict XVI 2011),
  joining Monaco/Luxembourg/Guyana/Moldova/Aruba's reserved-plate cases.
- **When operating internationally, Vatican vehicles must display a
  separate elliptical "V" oval per the Geneva Convention on Road
  Traffic** — a supranational-treaty-mandated identifier distinct from
  the plate itself, the same legal mechanism behind ordinary country
  ovals (D, F, GB, etc.) but explicit and sourced here.
