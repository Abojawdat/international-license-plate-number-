# Guatemala — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Guatemala), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/guatemala.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/guatemala.html).

- 300×152mm (NA standard), black on white since a Jan 2021 redesign.
  Format: 1 letter (vehicle type) + 3 digits + 3 letters. "GUATEMALA" +
  national emblem top, "CENTRAL AMERICA" bottom — same regional-bloc-text
  convention flagged in `costa-rica.md`.
- **Explicitly modeled after the Mercosur design and neighboring
  Honduras** per the source — cross-country design influence happening
  outside the formal Mercosur bloc membership, worth checking Honduras
  directly next given this direct pointer.
