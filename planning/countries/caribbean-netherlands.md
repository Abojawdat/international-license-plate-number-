# Caribbean Netherlands — evidence dossier

**First pass, richly documented per-island case.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Caribbean_Netherlands).

- 152×300mm. **Each of the 3 islands gets an oversized single-letter
  identifier, its own slogan, and its own regional imagery**: Bonaire
  (`B`, "Divers Paradise," flora/architecture/flamingos across the lower
  third), St. Eustatius (`E`, "The Historic Gem"), Saba (`S`, "The
  Unspoiled Queen") — a 3-island-in-1-territory customization case as
  granular as Turks and Caicos', and older (this one dates to the design's
  origin, not a 2022 redesign).
- Non-passenger categories: `AB` buses, `BF` mopeds, `D` government, `H`
  rental, `MF` motorcycles, `TX` taxis, `V` trucks, `Z` heavy equipment —
  a full letter-based category system matching Curaçao's (both are former
  Netherlands Antilles constituents, unsurprising given the shared
  administrative lineage).
- **1976 plates featured the official US Bicentennial Commission logo** —
  a foreign country's own national commemorative symbol borrowed onto a
  Caribbean territory's plate, the only cross-national commemorative
  borrowing found in any dossier.
