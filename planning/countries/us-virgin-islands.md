# US Virgin Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass, unusually deep slogan-era history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_United_States_Virgin_Islands). This is a US territory — cross-reference against `us-states/_tracker.md`, which already lists USVI briefly; this dossier goes deeper.

- Current (2023+): 300×150mm aluminum, `ABC 123`, island-coded.
- **5 distinct named-slogan eras**: "Tropical Playground" (1952-62),
  "Vacation Adventure" (1963-67), "American Paradise" (1968-2000), then
  unnamed 2000-2023 eras (yellow, beach-scene, ocean-graphic), and the
  current "175th Emancipation" design (March 2023) commemorating a
  specific historical anniversary — the deepest slogan-history dossier
  found, and the clearest case of a plate slogan being tied to a specific
  historical commemoration date rather than a generic place-marketing
  phrase.
- Island-coding since 1939: `C`=St. Croix, `T`=St. Thomas/St. John, `J`
  added separately for St. John in 1975 — a sub-territory split that
  itself changed granularity over time (one island gaining its own code
  36 years after the scheme started).
