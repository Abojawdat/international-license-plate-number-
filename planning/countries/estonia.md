# Estonia — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage, deep history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Estonia).

- 520×110mm, `123 ABC`. Categories: white/black standard, white/black
  classic (inverted-emphasis naming, `A9`), white/red racing, black/green
  mopeds, red/white transit, yellow temporary, white/black Defense Forces.
- **1907: among the earliest registration schemes found in any dossier**,
  predating most of this project's other "current since" dates by decades;
  early plates changed color annually — a historical precedent for
  Aruba's modern annual-color-rotation mechanism, worth noting it's not
  unprecedented.
- Soviet era: only 2 suffixes (`EA`, `ES`) marked Estonian-SSR
  registration, no city-level detail — a coarser-than-usual predecessor
  scheme.
- 1991-2019: first letter = registration office (A=Tallinn, T=Tartu);
  **August 2019: switched to fully random series** — the clearest example
  found yet of Spain's "deliberately drop regional meaning" direction,
  here dated precisely.
