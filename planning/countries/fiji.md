# Fiji — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Fiji).

- 372×134mm (Australian standard, matching the pattern-table bucket).
  Categories since 1976: general issue, privately-imported (no longer
  issued but still valid on the road — a "discontinued but still legal"
  status worth modeling explicitly, not just current vs. legacy), motorcycle
  (184×95mm), government (white/blue).
- Blanks and dies manufactured in Australia — another manufacturing-
  heritage detail (joining Angola/Rwanda/Zambia's colonial-tie findings,
  here a regional-neighbor tie instead).
- Serial progress is trackable/predictable (reached OK-999 by mid-2025) —
  same kind of literal-exhaustion-tracking as Trinidad and Tobago.
