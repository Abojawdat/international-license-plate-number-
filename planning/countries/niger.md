# Niger — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Niger).

- 520×110mm, `1A2345` since 2005, 8 numeric region codes. Black/white
  private, red-on-orange commercial (matches Republic of Congo/Cameroon's
  orange-family palettes), blue/white state transport, white/black
  military, orange/green diplomatic.
- Country code `RN` + a map of Niger in flag colors or black silhouette on
  the right — a country-outline graphic used as a design element (joins
  Nigeria's/DRC's/Cuba's flag-watermark-style approaches), here explicitly
  offered in two alternate treatments (colored or silhouette).
