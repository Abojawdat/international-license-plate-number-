# Moldova — evidence dossier

**Second pass, category gap mostly closed, one head-of-state finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Moldova), plus a Moldovan legal/insurance blog (blog.omnis.md) that specifically decodes special series letters — exactly the detail Wikipedia's article was missing.

- 520×110mm, `ABC 123`, black/white, blue euroband (state coat of arms on
  blue since Nov 2011). First issued Nov 30, 1992.
- Pre-2015: 7-character format (2 region letters + 2 series letters + 3
  digits) — a deeper regional-code layer than the current system uses.
- **Government/officials distinguished by series letters, not colors**:
  `RMG` = Government, `RMP` = Parliament, `RMA`/`MAI` = ministries —
  category encoded entirely through letter-series assignment, with no
  color change at all. Diplomatic: `CD` prefix, blue lettering, redesigned
  2012 to align with international diplomatic-plate norms.
- **The President's official car uses plate `001`** — a specific numeric
  reservation (not a letter prefix) plus the round state emblem, joining
  Monaco/Guyana/Luxembourg/Aruba's head-of-state category, and the most
  literal "lowest number reserved for the top office" case found.
- Taxi category: still not found in either pass — genuinely absent from
  available sources so far, not for lack of a targeted search.
