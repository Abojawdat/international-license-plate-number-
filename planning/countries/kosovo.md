# Kosovo — evidence dossier

**First pass, confirms the political-conflict pattern from Bosnia.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Kosovo).

- Current `RKS` system since Dec 6, 2010, replacing UNMIK-era `KS` plates
  (1999-2010) — Kosovo's own predecessor format was itself a UN
  administrative one, not a prior sovereign design.
- 520×110mm, `01 234-AB`, blue field with gold "RKS."
- Categories: police (red font), Kosovo Security Force (dark green/
  yellow), NATO/KFOR (blue/white, `KFOR` prefix — a foreign military
  force getting its own plate category is new), export (blue/white),
  historic (`HISTORIK` text).
- **A 2022 plate-standardization requirement led to real protests and
  ongoing conflict in Serbian-majority northern Kosovo**, with
  implementation delayed a month after US/EU consultation — a second
  confirmed case (after Bosnia) of plate policy being a live political
  flashpoint, not just an administrative detail. See `bosnia-and-
  herzegovina.md` for the fuller note on why this matters for the
  tracker's approach to politically sensitive countries.
