## What's known
- Format since 1997: black text on white; province name in Arabic on the
  left, Latin-character numbering below it — a stacked bilingual-ish layout,
  though this reads more like "province name in Arabic + numbers in Latin
  script" than Saudi's full dual-script-of-the-same-content pattern.
- Category colors: white = standard, yellow = taxi, blue = private security
  companies. Government/diplomatic/security-force plates exist with
  distinct codes/colors, not yet detailed.
- **Dimension conflict**: this search round returned "440 × 300 mm ... for
  both front and rear plates," which is a very different aspect ratio from
  the earlier bulk comparative table's "Syria, Tunisia: 450×100 mm (front
  plates)" figure logged in `_tracker.md`. A 300mm-tall plate would be
  roughly square-ish and unlike anything else gathered so far — likely a
  scrape error (possibly conflating two plates stacked, or a units mixup)
  rather than a real design, but **not resolved**, flagged rather than
  picked.
