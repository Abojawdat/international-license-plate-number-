# Vietnam — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Vietnam), [Saigoneer explainer](https://saigoneer.com/saigon-culture/28533-what-can-vietnamese-license-plates-tell-you-about-the-vehicles-and-who-drives-them), a 2026 ALPR vendor writeup (hitech.com.tw) covering a 2023 owner-linked-plate policy change.

- Format `12A-345.67`: 2-digit province code + serial letter + digits.
- Colors: white = private, yellow = commercial/ride-hail, blue = government,
  red = military.
- **Every car is issued two physically different plates simultaneously**:
  a short one (330×165mm) and a long one (520×110mm) — not an
  either/or concurrent-format choice like Kuwait/Jordan/Morocco, but
  "both, always, same vehicle." A new variant of the multi-size finding.
- A 2023 policy links plates to the owner rather than the vehicle — worth
  noting as a business-logic detail, not a rendering one.
