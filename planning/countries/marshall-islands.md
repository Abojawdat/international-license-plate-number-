# Marshall Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass, one material finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Marshall_Islands).

- 152×305mm (NA standard). Numeric-only serial (`1234`).
- Original (1972, under US Trust Territory administration): black/white.
  Current: white/green, "MAJURO ATOLL" text — the capital atoll's name
  used as a fixed slogan/label, an unusually specific place-name choice
  for a whole-country plate.
- **Manufactured from silver or wood** — confirms Palau's wood-as-a-plate-
  material finding isn't a one-off; a real small-Pacific-island materials
  pattern worth expecting in any other Micronesian/Polynesian nation
  researched later.
