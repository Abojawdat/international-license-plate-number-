# Cape Verde — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Cape_Verde).

- 520×110mm, `AB-12-CD`, island-code prefix (8 islands, e.g. BR=Brava,
  SV=São Vicente) — region coding at the *island* level, a finer grain
  than most archipelago nations found so far.
- White/black standard, yellow/black government (`G` suffix), white/red
  diplomatic (`CD-12-345`), military (`FA-12-34`), green/red-with-white
  special vehicles.
- Predecessor: Portuguese Overseas Territories system, `CVS-`/`CVB-`
  prefixes splitting the archipelago into Sotavento/Barlavento island
  groups rather than individual islands — a coarser-to-finer regional
  granularity change over time, the opposite direction from Slovakia's
  code elimination.
