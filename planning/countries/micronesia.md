# Federated States of Micronesia — evidence dossier

**First pass, confirms Palau's granular-federation pattern.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Federated_States_of_Micronesia).

- 152×305mm (NA standard), numeric-only. **4 states (Chuuk, Kosrae,
  Pohnpei, Yap) each issue their own fully distinct plate design** — a
  2nd confirmed Pacific case of state-level federation within a single
  small country (after Palau), reinforcing finding #50 as a real pattern
  in this region, not Palau alone.
- Kosrae: blue/white, "DIVERS HAVEN." Pohnpei: white/green. Yap:
  black/white, "ISLAND OF STONE MONEY" — a slogan referencing Yap's
  famous large-stone currency, among the most culturally specific slogans
  found in any dossier.
- Chuuk (as Truk) had plates as early as 1964; current 4-state series
  since 2000.
