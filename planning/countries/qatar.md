## What's known
- Dimensions: 520 × 110 mm standard; a wider "30.3 × 15 cm" variant reported
  for heavier vehicles — a second concurrent-size case, same pattern already
  seen in Kuwait and Jordan (see `architecture-notes.md` finding #5).
- Private: maroon and white.
- Taxi (Karwa): metallic grey background, black text, bilingual "Taxi" label.
- Private commercial transport (pickups/vans/minibuses): black plates.
- Government: two descriptions given — green background/white text, *or*
  black-and-white with "GOV." text. **Possible conflict or two different
  government sub-categories** — not resolved.
- Police: blue and white.
- Security force ("Lekhwiya"): yellow background, red text.
