# Malta — evidence dossier

**First pass, deep history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Malta).

- Current (1995-present): 520×110mm, `ZZZ 999`, black/white, blue EU
  strip with "M".
- Category encoded by a *suffix letter pattern* rather than a prefix:
  ending in K/QZ = rental, `PY` = buses/coaches — a suffix-based category
  encoding, the mirror image of every prefix-based case found so far.
  Taxis use literal "TAXI" text in the serial itself.
- Vintage/classic: silver on black — joins the growing historic-vehicle-
  gets-its-own-color list (Czech, France, Serbia below).
- Deep 3-era history: 1952-1979 (5-digit, color-coded by category), 1979-
  1995 (letter-hyphen-number), 1995-present.
