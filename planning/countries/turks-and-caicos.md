# Turks and Caicos Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass, one sub-national customization finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Turks_and_Caicos_Islands).

- 152×300mm. Categories: black/white passenger, green/white-with-green-
  border commercial, black/white government, yellow/white-with-yellow-
  border self-drive rental, red-on-gradient-blue/white/green taxi,
  Governor's vehicle (crown symbol only — same British-overseas-territory
  Governor category as Cayman Islands).
- **Dec 2022 redesign gives each island its own distinct graphic** — a
  sub-national customization *within* one small territory's single
  official format, a more granular version of Mexico's/Canada's
  province-level design variance, here happening at the individual-island
  level within one already-small country.
- History: 1970s embossed white-on-black → 2000 redesign (coat of arms,
  "Beautiful by Nature" slogan) → Dec 2022 (security features + per-island
  graphics).
