# Greece — evidence dossier

**First pass, one genuinely novel finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Greece).

- 520×110mm. Current since 2004: `ABC-1234`.
- **Letters are restricted to the subset of Latin capitals that are
  shaped identically to a Greek capital letter** (Α, Β, Ε, Ζ, Η, Ι, Κ, Μ,
  Ν, Ο, Ρ, Τ, Υ, Χ) — a third distinct exclusion-reason category, after
  visual-confusion-within-one-script (Saudi/Russia/Egypt/Pakistan/Malaysia/
  Poland) and cultural-taboo (Taiwan's excluded 4). This one is
  *cross-script visual compatibility* — the plate is meant to read
  correctly whether the viewer parses it as Latin or Greek.
- Motorcycles: square plates, numbers only, 1-999.
- Athens/Thessaloniki get single-letter prefecture codes, other regions
  get two — same population-driven code-length rule as Germany's district
  codes.
- Deep format history: 5 distinct eras from 1911 to 2004 (numbers-only →
  city-initials-yellow → six-digit-only → two-letter → three-letter),
  comparable in depth to Kuwait's/DRC's multi-era findings.
