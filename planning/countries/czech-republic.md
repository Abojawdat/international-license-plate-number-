# Czech Republic — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Czech_Republic).

- 520×110mm. Format `1A2 3456`, region letter drawn from A-Z (excluding O,
  Q, W, G, CH) tied to each region's capital (A=Prague, B=Brno, C=České
  Budějovice, P=Plzeň, etc.).
- Historic vehicles: `V` prefix, format `NNV XXXX` — green letters on
  white (a distinct color for the historic category, not just a prefix).
- Colors: black/white standard, black/yellow commercial/heavy goods, blue/
  white diplomatic (post-2001), military = numbers only, no letters.
- Since mid-2023 Prague plates added an extra letter to the serial — a very
  recent, city-specific capacity extension (same family as Sweden/
  Netherlands/Panama's capacity-driven changes, but scoped to one city
  rather than the whole country).
- Current system: 2001, replacing a 1954 district-based one; EU strip added
  2004 on EU accession.
