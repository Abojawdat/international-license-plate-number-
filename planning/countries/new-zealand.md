# New Zealand — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_New_Zealand).

- 360×124mm, `AAA100`. Black/white standard; dealer/trade black/yellow.
- Current 3-letter system since April 2001, replacing a 2-letter format
  that ran out of combinations (capacity-exhaustion, same family as Sweden/
  Netherlands/Panama).
- Nov 1986: switched from silver-on-black to black-on-white — an inversion
  matching the pattern already seen in DR Congo/Senegal/Portugal's era
  transitions.
- **2022: optional black plates reintroduced, with an "NZ" hologram
  specifically so officers can confirm validity** — an aesthetic/nostalgia
  option (not a category) paired with a digital anti-fraud feature, a
  combination not seen in quite this form elsewhere.
