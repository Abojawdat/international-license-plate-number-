# Netherlands — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Netherlands), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/netherlands.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/netherlands.html).

- **Yellow background is the default for ordinary plates** — an outlier
  vs. the white-default majority (joins Oman/Malaysia/Cameroon as
  non-white/non-standard defaults, though yellow specifically hasn't come
  up as a *default* before, only as a category color elsewhere).
- 520×110mm or 340×210mm; motorcycle 210×143mm — another multi-size country.
- **7 different serial format patterns in rotation** (`A-001-AA`,
  `AA-001-A`, `00-AAA-1`, etc.) as the letter/digit combinations get used
  up over time — a live illustration of Panama's capacity-exhaustion
  finding, except here it's planned rotation through known patterns rather
  than a one-time forced redesign.
- Blue left band: EU flag + "NL".
