# Somaliland — evidence dossier

**First pass, breakaway-territory dossier — flag for the political
sensitivity conversation already noted in `_tracker.md` before building.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Somaliland).

- Current (2005 series): black/white, "S.LAND" shown in **both Latin and
  Arabic script** — a bilingual state-name label similar in kind to
  Cambodia's/Nepal's bilingual region labels, here at the country-identity
  level for a state most of the world doesn't recognize.
- Government: white/green, full "SOMALILAND" spelled out. Foreign/
  humanitarian aid vehicles: white/red, "S.LAND" — aid vehicles getting
  their own dedicated category is a new case (distinct from UN/NGO
  categories already seen, which are usually folded into diplomatic).
- 1996: first independent system, spelled out "SOMALILAND" in both
  scripts; replaced by the current abbreviated 2005 series.
