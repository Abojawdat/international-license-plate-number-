# Australia — evidence dossier

**Status: first pass, architecturally important.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Australia) plus a state-by-state explainer (carify.com.au).

## What's known
- **Explicitly confirmed as federated, no national system**: "Australia
  doesn't have one national system, but eight" (six states + two
  territories), each with its own format, colors, and slogan. The clearest,
  most direct confirmation yet of the federated-country pattern (after US,
  UAE, South Africa, Mexico).
- Dimensions: 372×135mm standard (matches the pattern-table's Australia/
  Pacific-Rim bucket exactly), 210×135mm for motorcycles.
- Per-state detail gathered: Queensland (3 digits + 2 letters + 1 digit,
  never 0/1 in the last slot, "SUNSHINE STATE" embossed), Victoria
  ("THE EDUCATION STATE" screened), Tasmania (thylacine logo left,
  "Explore the possibilities"), Western Australia (blue/white,
  `#LLL-###`) — each state's slogan is literally part of the plate design,
  a decorative-text element with no equivalent modeled anywhere yet.
