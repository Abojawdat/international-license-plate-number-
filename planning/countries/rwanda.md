# Rwanda — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Rwanda).

- 520×110mm. Front black/white, rear black/yellow. Format `RXX ###X`.
- Made with **Belgian stamping dies** — a manufacturing-heritage detail
  (colonial/historical ties shaping who actually produces the plates),
  same pattern as Angola (Portuguese) and Zambia (German, below).
