# Guernsey — evidence dossier

**First pass, real-money cherished-plate finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Guernsey).

- 520×110mm, up to 5 digits, **no letters at all** — purely numeric,
  joining Algeria/NWT/Nunavut's numeric-only cases.
- `1`: reserved for the Bailiff of Guernsey. `G1`/`G2`: Lieutenant
  Governor's private vehicles — 2 distinct reserved-office categories.
- **A low-digit plate ("007") sold at auction for £240,000 in Sept 2015**
  — concrete evidence that desirable registrations carry real, large
  secondary-market value in some jurisdictions. Worth remembering for the
  business side of a paid SDK: a "which numbers/plates are desirable"
  feature isn't a hypothetical nice-to-have everywhere, it's a real market
  in places like Guernsey (and Jersey, see below).
- 2012: plates starting `0`/`00` released specifically to generate
  government revenue — a deliberate monetization-driven release strategy,
  a new "why issue this now" reason distinct from format/redesign history.
