# Barbados — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage, one novel naming finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Barbados).

- **Parish letters follow saints' names** (with `X` for Christ Church) —
  a region-code naming basis rooted in religious/ecclesiastical geography,
  not administrative boundaries — a new basis for region codes, distinct
  from every population/political-boundary-driven scheme found so far.
- Rich category table: minibuses (blue/white), public buses (BM), rental
  (blue/white), limousines (white/red), government/parliament (white/
  green, `MP` prefix), taxis (blue/white, `Z` prefix), private buses
  (`ZR`), motor dealers (red/white), diplomatic (yellow/blue), Presidential
  (black with metallic emblem — a head-of-state category again, joining
  Guyana's).
- Dual-letter prefixes kick in once a parish's single-letter range is
  exhausted — another capacity-driven variance, scoped per-parish.
