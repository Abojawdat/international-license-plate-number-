# Austria — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Austria).

- 520×120mm (taller than the usual 110mm). District codes: 1 letter for
  state capitals, 2 for others (same population-driven rule as Germany).
  Excludes Q.
- EV plates get a green stripe added on the right (yet another distinct
  EV-encoding mechanism — a stripe on a specific side, different from
  Indonesia's expiry-row trim or Norway's prefix-letter-set below).
- Since 2002: EU blue strip + "A".
