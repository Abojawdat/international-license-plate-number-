# Singapore — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Singapore), LTA-approval guides (carplatemaker.sg, samedaycarplate.sg).

- **Two entirely different concurrent physical formats**: rectangular
  single-line 520×110mm (Euro-style, black/white front + black/yellow
  rear) and squarish two-line 325×160mm — a real coexisting-layout case,
  not just a coexisting-size one (the two-line format isn't just a resized
  single-line, it's a different row structure), similar in kind to Kuwait's
  square motorcycle blank.
- Exact character sizing specified by regulation (50mm tall, 10mm stroke,
  30mm total width per character) — LTA-governed like UK's Charles Wright
  spec.
