# Republic of the Congo — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Republic_of_the_Congo). Distinct from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (`dr-congo.md`) — a separate country.

- 520×110mm. Format `123AB4`/`123A45` — district code + series letters +
  area code, based on the French FNI system since 1982.
- 14 region codes (1=Nkayi ... 14=Plateau).
- Colors: black/white standard, orange-on-green diplomatic, black-on-green
  temporary NGO imports (`123AB4 IT` suffix — an explicit import-purpose
  tag baked into the plate, not just a color).
- CEMAC (Central African Economic and Monetary Community) emblem on all
  plates since 2006 — a shared regional-bloc emblem requirement, similar in
  spirit to the Mercosur bloc's shared branding but here layered onto an
  otherwise country-specific design rather than a fully shared blank.
