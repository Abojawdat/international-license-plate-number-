# Timor-Leste — evidence dossier

**First pass, deep colonial/occupation history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_East_Timor).

- Current since 2002 independence: 372×134mm (Australian standard,
  Australian dies — joins Fiji/Samoa/Vanuatu's regional-neighbor
  manufacturing tie), `TL`/`TLS` code (Tetum "Timor Lorosae" or
  Portuguese "Timor-Leste" — bilingual country abbreviation itself).
  Government: `G`-suffixed.
- 3-era colonial/political history: Portuguese Timor (`T`/`TP`, white on
  black), Indonesian occupation 1975-1999 (`DF` letters, expiry dates
  shown), independence-era current system — a format history directly
  tracking colonial and occupation transitions, one of the most explicitly
  politically-driven histories found.
