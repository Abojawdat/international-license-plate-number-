# Cayman Islands — evidence dossier

**First pass, one novel "why redesigned" finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Cayman_Islands).

- 152×300mm, "Cayman Islands" text beneath a 6-digit grouped serial.
- Full category table: black/white standard/hire cars, black/orange heavy
  goods, red/white taxi, red/yellow disabled drivers, black/yellow private
  cars, Governor's vehicle (crown emblem on black, front only — a British-
  overseas-territory Governor category, a colonial-administrative
  head-of-territory case distinct from a head-of-state).
- **2003 quincentennial "Q-plates" were issued with white characters on
  scenic backgrounds, then recalled and replaced specifically because the
  white characters weren't visible enough** — the first confirmed case of
  a plate being redesigned due to a legibility production failure, a new
  "why did the design change" reason distinct from capacity, politics,
  crime-prevention, or cross-border misreading.
