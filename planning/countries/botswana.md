# Botswana — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Botswana).

- `B` + 3 digits + 3 letters, Q excluded (confusable with O). Front white/
  black, rear yellow/black (face-color-split, joins UK/HK/Kenya/etc.).
- Government: `BX` prefix, inverted colors front vs rear (red-on-white
  front, white-on-red rear — a category with its own face-inversion rule).
  Defence force: `BDF` prefix, non-reflective green.
- Historical Protectorate-era location codes (BPA=Francistown,
  BPD=Gaborone, etc.) persisted after independence — administrative
  continuity across a sovereignty change, a new kind of format-history
  driver.
