# Vanuatu — evidence dossier

**First pass, one unique finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Vanuatu).

- 372×134mm (Australian standard), `ABCD 1`. White/black standard,
  red/green diplomatic. A palm-tree side decoration on one design variant.
- **The government does not manufacture or supply plates at all — it is
  the vehicle owner's legal duty to have one made** — the only confirmed
  case of plate production being entirely the citizen's responsibility
  rather than a government or licensed-manufacturer process. A real
  outlier against the implicit assumption (in every other dossier) that
  a government or its licensed vendor issues the physical plate.
- No official country code or slogan on the plate.
