# Aruba — evidence dossier

**First pass, one major architectural finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Aruba).

- 520×110mm, `A 1234`. **Issues an entirely new plate every year with a
  different rotating color combination, instead of a validity sticker** —
  a wholly new mechanism for showing current registration: the *year is
  the color*, not a separate mark. Distinct from the Dominican Republic's
  semi-annual alternating-color reissue (which was twice yearly, tracked
  differently) — this is annual and the color itself carries no other
  meaning, purely a rotating validity signal.
- **Governor's vehicle uses the reserved `A-1` plate** — another head-of-
  state-specific plate (joins Guyana's President/PM, Luxembourg's Grand-
  Ducal plates below).
- 1986: dropped "N.A." (Netherlands Antilles) designation after gaining
  autonomous status — a sovereignty-status-driven text change.
- 2019: adopted FE-Schrift for counterfeit resistance — 5th+ country with
  this typeface now.
