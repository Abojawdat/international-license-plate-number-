# Kyrgyzstan — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Kyrgyzstan).

- 520×110mm, `01 123ABC`. Black/white standard and government (shared
  color, distinguished only by context/prefix), black/yellow foreign,
  white/red consular, white/blue UN.
- Soviet-era plates (from 1980) continued in use until 1994 despite 1991
  independence — a real "old regime's format outlives the political
  transition by years" case, useful context for how format-history
  timelines don't always align cleanly with political history.
- July 2015: two-digit regional codes added, explicitly modeled on
  Russia's system — another cross-country design-influence case.
