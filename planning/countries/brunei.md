# Brunei — evidence dossier

**First pass, one unique finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Brunei).

- Two concurrent car sizes (216×343mm, 533×152mm) plus two motorcycle
  sizes — another multi-size country.
- Format historically tied prefix letter to district (`B`=Brunei-Muara,
  `K`=Kuala Belait), but **since 2006 `K` applies regardless of actual
  registration location** — a region code that became purely nominal after
  a policy change, a live example of France's "cosmetic region code"
  finding happening *over time* rather than by original design.
- **Royal family has its own plate category** (color varies, e.g. red) —
  a monarchy-specific category with no equivalent in any other dossier so
  far.
- I/O excluded (visual confusion); `D` reserved/restricted so `BD` plates
  don't exist — a specific letter-combination exclusion beyond single-
  character rules.
