# Canada — sub-tracker

**Status: first pass, all 13 provinces/territories, single source.**
Same treatment as `us-states/_tracker.md` — replaces trying to fit Canada
into one Iraq-shaped dossier.

## Source
[Vehicle registration plates of Canada — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Canada).

## Baseline (applies to all 13 unless noted)
- 150×300mm, same 1956 standardization agreement as the US (see
  `us-states/_tracker.md` — the US/Canada blank is one shared standard, not
  two coincidentally-matching ones).
- I, O, Q, U excluded nationally for visual confusion — **British Columbia
  additionally excludes Y and Z** except on motorcycles, a province-specific
  extension of the national rule.
- Northwest Territories and Nunavut use **all-numeric serials only** — no
  letters at all, joining Algeria as a confirmed numeric-only case.
- Only BC, Manitoba, and Ontario require both front and rear plates — every
  other province/territory is rear-only. Already logged in
  `architecture-notes.md` finding #22 from this same source; this table
  just gives the per-province specifics.
- Validation stickers are being phased out in favor of camera-based
  enforcement in most provinces — Quebec dropped stickers in 1992,
  Saskatchewan in 2012.

## What this adds

1. **Newfoundland and Labrador's plates are explicitly vehicle-bound**,
   stated directly in contrast to Cuba/Belgium's driver-bound plates
   (finding #17/#27) — useful as a clean confirmed baseline case now that
   both ends of that axis are documented.
2. **Nunavut's bear-shaped-to-rectangular reversion is a second confirmed
   format oscillation**, alongside Slovenia's — not a one-off.
3. Every province/territory's exact serial format wasn't detailed further
   in this source (just "alphanumeric") — this table is a real first pass,
   not a deep one; a second source or photos would be needed to get to the
   letter/digit pattern precision the US table already has.
