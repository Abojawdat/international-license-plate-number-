# El Salvador — evidence dossier

**Second pass, category gap closed via a different source family.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_El_Salvador) stays a stub, but El Salvador's own transport regulation (Reglamento General de Transporte Terrestre) turned up in a targeted search — category rules exist in law even though the encyclopedia article never got expanded to cover them.

- 152×300mm (NA standard), current design since 2011. El Salvador flag in
  background. Format `A-1234` (standard), `M-1-234` (motorcycles).
- **Taxis: solid yellow, plus a mandatory black-and-white checkered stripe
  (30cm wide) around the vehicle body** with a "Taxi" legend — the
  category marking extends past the plate itself onto the vehicle body,
  the first confirmed case of a category rule governing more than the
  plate and its immediate surface.
- Private vehicles are legally barred from using yellow, or yellow with
  black/white striping, specifically because those combinations are
  reserved for transport categories — an exclusion rule on the *private*
  category to protect the *taxi* category's visual identity, the inverse
  of every other exclusion reason found (those all protect legibility or
  cultural sensitivity, not another category's exclusivity).
- A vehicle leaving taxi service can keep the yellow color once the
  taxi stripe is removed — color and category are not permanently locked
  together here.
