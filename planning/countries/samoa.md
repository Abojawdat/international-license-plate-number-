# Samoa — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Samoa).

- 372×134mm (Australian standard). Numeric-only serial.
- 3-era color history (green/white 1950s → green/black 1979-2000 →
  white/blue 2000-present).
- 2000: adopted Australian/NZ stamping dies, including **a slash through
  the zero character** — a typographic convention (distinguishing 0 from
  O) worth remembering for `PlateTypeface` if Samoa or similar countries
  are ever built.
- "Penina ole Pasefika" (Pearl of the Pacific) slogan since 2000 — joins
  the fixed-slogan list.
