## What's known
- Dimensions: 520 × 110 mm.
- Private: black text on yellow — **notably different from every other GCC
  country gathered so far**, which default private plates to white. Worth
  double-checking since it's an outlier compared to Jordan/Kuwait/Saudi/
  Qatar/Bahrain, all white-background private plates.
- Taxi: red plates.
- Government: white background, red text, Arabic حكومة ("Government") label.
- A long category list is claimed: Private, Taxi, Commercial, Learner,
  Government, Political Body, Authority, Consulate, UN, Tractor, Bicycle,
  Export, Checking, Rental — comparable in scale to Kuwait's confirmed
  12+ category list. Not yet cross-checked against a second source or
  Commons photos the way Kuwait's was.
