# Mozambique — evidence dossier

**First pass, one rendering-technique finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Mozambique).

- Current (2011+): `ABC 123 [crest] DE`, single or two-row. 11 province
  codes (2-letter, e.g. MC=Maputo City).
- **Background is a gradient — white at top fading to light blue at
  bottom** — the first confirmed gradient-fill background found; every
  other dossier's field colors are flat. Two crossing gray wavy lines as a
  security feature (a guilloche-adjacent pattern, similar in spirit to
  Iraq's own guilloche).
- Pre-2011: Portuguese-colonial `MXX-99-99` format, white on black —
  another Portuguese-heritage predecessor format (joins Angola).
