# Saint Lucia — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Saint_Lucia).

- 300×152mm, `WL` code. White/black standard, white/blue taxi (`TX`),
  white/green minibus, black/yellow government (`SLG`).
- **Taxi/minibus plates sometimes show the vehicle's licensed passenger
  capacity directly on the plate** — a capacity/licensing number embedded
  in the plate itself, a new field type not seen elsewhere.
- Older plates were owner-provided in varying styles before
  standardization — same predecessor pattern as several Pacific islands'
  owner-manufactured eras, here in the Caribbean.
