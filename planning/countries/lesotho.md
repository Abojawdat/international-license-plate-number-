# Lesotho — evidence dossier

**First pass, one culturally-specific finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Lesotho).

- `A 1234`. Blue/white private, red/white government, blue/white
  diplomatic (`CD`), red/white dealer.
- **A "mokorotlo" sticker** (the traditional Basotho conical hat, a
  national symbol appearing on Lesotho's flag) **matching the plate's
  character color indicates a 5-year validity period** — the first
  confirmed validity mechanism tied to a specific cultural/national symbol
  rather than a generic sticker, hologram, or color-rotation.
- Pre-1979: white/black, `L`-prefix. Diplomatic corps historically used
  distinctive country-specific codes (`UKHC`, `USA`, `WHO`) "during a
  period of dispute among foreign legations" — a politically-contentious
  diplomatic-plate episode, a smaller-scale version of the Bosnia/Kosovo
  political-plate pattern but involving foreign missions rather than
  domestic ethnic groups.
