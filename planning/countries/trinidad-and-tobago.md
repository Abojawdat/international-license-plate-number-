# Trinidad and Tobago — evidence dossier

**First pass, one unique finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Trinidad_and_Tobago).

- **The law explicitly lets owners choose between white-on-black or
  black-on-white**, based on preference/vehicle color — the only dossier
  found so far where plate color is a *personal choice* rather than
  determined by category, region, or propulsion type. A genuinely new axis
  the palette-resolution model doesn't account for at all.
- Category by prefix letter: P=private, T=commercial/goods, H=taxi/hired,
  R=rental, D=dealer-demo, V=visitor (rare), X=heavy equipment.
- Excludes I, V, O, Q (visual confusion, but also — V is excluded as a
  letter *and* used as a category prefix, worth double-checking that's not
  a source contradiction).
- Two physical layouts: rectangular single-row and **square two-row** —
  another concurrent-layout country (Kuwait/Singapore pattern).
- Letter-series progression (single digit → PA-PZ → PAA-PAZ...) is
  literally trackable — as of 2026, on series "EM."
