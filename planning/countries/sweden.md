# Sweden — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Sweden).

- 520×110mm standard; American-style 300×111mm and a **square** 119×155mm
  motorcycle variant also exist — another multi-size, and another
  non-rectangular-in-spirit case (joining Kuwait/Singapore's square
  formats).
- Format `ABC 123`/`ABC 12A`. **2019 change allowed the last character to
  be a letter or digit** specifically to prevent registration exhaustion —
  a 3rd confirmed capacity-exhaustion-driven format change (after Panama's
  forced redesign and the Netherlands' rotating-pattern approach); Sweden's
  is the most surgical version, changing one character's rules rather than
  the whole format.
- Colors: black/white standard, black/yellow taxi, black/blue diplomatic,
  white/red temporary (with expiry date shown), black/green dealer/test
  vehicles (not tied to a specific car — similar in spirit to Cuba's
  person-bound-not-vehicle-bound flag, worth cross-referencing), yellow/
  black military (4-6 digits only, no letters).
- EU stripe mandatory since Jan 2014.
