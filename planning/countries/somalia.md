# Somalia — evidence dossier

**First pass, fragmented-state finding, deep colonial history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Somalia).

- No comprehensive current national plate spec found — matches Somalia's
  actual fractured governance. **Puntland (a de facto autonomous region
  since 1998) issues its own plates independently**: 2 letters + 3 digits
  + Arabic numerals lower-right, "PUNTLAND" in Latin and Arabic, regional
  coat of arms — the same shape of governance fragmentation already seen
  in Yemen (Houthi/government/STC split) and Libya's post-2011 instability,
  but here it's produced a genuinely separate, internally-consistent
  regional design rather than just inconsistency.
- Deep colonial format history: 1913-1920s Italian colonial plates (white,
  red colony markings), 1927-1935 (white-on-black, colonial code + up to 5
  digits), 1937-1941 (green/white/red tricolor squares, `AOI` for Italian
  East Africa) — a format history driven entirely by colonial
  administrative changes, similar in kind to Timor-Leste's occupation-
  driven history.
- Somaliland (the other breakaway region, already flagged in `_tracker.md`
  for political sensitivity) not covered by this source at all — needs its
  own separate check.
