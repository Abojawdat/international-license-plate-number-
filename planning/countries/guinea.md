# Guinea — evidence dossier

**First pass, good history depth.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Guinea).

- Current (2021+): `AB-123 [##]`, blue/white for every category (no
  per-category color differentiation found — categories may exist without
  color distinction here, similar shape to Poland's category-replaces-
  region-slot but unconfirmed).
- **2021: switched region codes from letters (e.g. `RC` for Conakry) to
  numbers (`02`)** — a letter-to-number region-code type change, the
  reverse direction of most region-code evolutions found (which add or
  drop detail, not change the *type* of code).
- Security: QR code + flag-map graphic. Previous (1994-2021): white/red
  passenger, white/black taxi/truck — the color scheme was dropped
  entirely in the 2021 redesign in favor of uniform blue/white, a
  category-collapsing redesign (fewer distinct colors than before, not
  more).
