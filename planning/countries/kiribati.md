# Kiribati — evidence dossier

**First pass, confirms the Pacific self-manufacture pattern a 3rd time.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Kiribati).

- `ABC 123`, white/black. **Owner-manufactured, hand-painted/stencilled/
  printed-laminated-paper-on-wood** — identical rule and even identical
  wording to Vanuatu's finding, now a 3rd Pacific nation (with Tonga)
  confirmed doing this, making it a real regional pattern rather than
  Vanuatu's one-off (finding #38 should be reframed as "a Pacific-islands
  pattern," not a single-country exception).
- No country code displayed at all — joins Djibouti as a 2nd confirmed
  case of skipping the country identifier entirely.
- Enhanced variant adds the Kiribati coat of arms on the left — an
  optional/upgraded design tier over the basic version, not tied to a
  vehicle category.
