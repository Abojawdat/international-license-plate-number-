# Central African Republic — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Central_African_Republic).

- `LL CCC LL` with letters restricted to specific ranges (DA-SZ, UB-VZ) —
  an unusually precise letter-range restriction, more specific than a
  simple exclusion list.
- White/black regular, CEMAC emblem (joins Republic of Congo/Gabon's
  CEMAC-bloc branding). Red/white temporary. Green diplomatic, with 3
  distinct sub-formats for ambassadors vs. other diplomats vs. consular
  staff (`CMD`/`CD`/`K` suffixes) — a 3-tier diplomatic hierarchy encoded
  directly in the format, more granular than most.
- 3-era history: black/silver (earliest) → blue background (1980s) →
  white/black CEMAC-standardized (since Sept 1, 2006).
