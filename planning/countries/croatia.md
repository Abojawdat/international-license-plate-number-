# Croatia — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Croatia).

- 520×110mm, `AB 123-CD`, coat of arms separates region code from serial.
  **Excludes Q, W, X, Y because they're not in the Croatian alphabet** —
  the same underlying principle as Greece/Ukraine/Bulgaria/Serbia's
  cross-script-compatibility exclusions, but framed as "not in our own
  alphabet" rather than "compatible with another script" — worth folding
  into the same exclusion-reason category rather than treating as new.
- Rich categories: green (foreign residents/international orgs/temporary),
  red (oversized vehicles), blue/white police, yellow/black military
  (`HV` prefix), blue/yellow diplomatic, green/yellow export.
- Design derives from 1980s Yugoslav plates; 2016 decision to keep it
  as-is (with an EU sticker added) rather than redesign after 2013 EU
  accession — a deliberate *non-change* despite pressure to redesign,
  worth remembering as a real outcome, not every EU-accession forces a
  redesign (contrast Bulgaria/Czech/Romania, which did redesign).
