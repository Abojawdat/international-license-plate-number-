# North Korea — evidence dossier

**First pass, two novel findings.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_North_Korea).

- Format loosely based on Japan's (numeric vehicle-type prefix).
- Colors: yellow/black private, blue/white state-owned, white-on-green
  diplomatic, white-on-red foreign-owned, white-on-black military, green
  Kŭmgangsan special economic zone, blue Red Cross, black UN.
- **Excludes the prefix "4"** — same cultural/superstition reason as
  Taiwan, confirming that exclusion-reason category (#19b) as a real
  cross-East-Asian pattern, not a one-off.
- **VIP plates encode "7•27" (July 27, Victory Day) at the start of the
  registration number** — a politically-symbolic date baked directly into
  a serial, a category of "meaning encoded in the number" not seen
  anywhere else (distinct from Ghana/Ireland's registration-date encoding,
  which is administrative, not symbolic).
- 1992: private plates expanded 4→5 digits — another capacity-driven
  change.
