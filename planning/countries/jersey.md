# Jersey — evidence dossier

**First pass, confirms Guernsey's cherished-plate economy.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Jersey).

- 520×111mm, `J` + 1-6 digits. Black/white front, black/yellow rear.
  Coat of arms + "GBJ" code — explicitly noted as *not* Vienna-Convention-
  valid despite looking EU-standard (not blue) — a plate that resembles
  an international standard without actually complying with the treaty
  behind it, worth flagging for anyone assuming "EU-shaped" implies
  "Vienna-Convention compliant."
- **Plate `J4` sold in 2020 for £380,000**, funding an EV purchase for
  Government House — an even larger confirmed cherished-plate sale than
  Guernsey's, and here the proceeds funded a specific real government
  purchase, not just general revenue.
- Since 1915, issued by Driver and Vehicle Standards (DVS).
