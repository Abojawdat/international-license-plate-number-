# Haiti — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage, deep history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Haiti).

- Current (2014-present): 300×152mm, pale-blue private/taxi/rental, pale-
  red public-transport/goods, pale-green diplomatic/international/tourist
  — a full pastel-toned category palette, distinct in tone from every
  other dossier's saturated colors.
- 2006-2013: single rear plate only, windshield sticker replaced the front
  plate, right-hand-drive vehicles banned — a genuinely unusual "one plate
  only" era (every other dossier assumes front+rear or rear-only as a
  fixed rule, not something that changed mid-history).
- 2014 redesign simultaneously **removed** regional designation and
  **added** color-coding by vehicle type — simplification and new
  complexity landing in the same redesign, worth noting these two
  directions aren't mutually exclusive within one format change.
- Slogan "La perle des Antilles" (Pearl of the Antilles) — joins Honduras'
  forest slogan and Australia's state slogans as fixed decorative text.
