# Jamaica — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Jamaica).

- 152×300mm. Private (since 1987): blue on white, `1234 AB`. Full category
  table: public transport (red/white), commercial (green/white), temporary
  (blue/white), rental (white/green, "RENTAL" text below), government
  (yellow/black, "JAMAICA GOVT" text below), military (black/white, `JDF`
  = Jamaica Defense Force), diplomatic (white/blue).
- **Military scheme explicitly described as borrowed from the British
  design also used in Trinidad and Tobago** — another cross-country
  design-influence case (joining Guatemala/Honduras/Mercosur), this one
  specifically a shared colonial/Commonwealth military-plate convention.
