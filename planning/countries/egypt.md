## What's known
- Format: numeric part (3 digits Cairo, 4 digits elsewhere) + alphabetic
  part (2 letters Giza, 3 letters elsewhere) — **governorate-dependent
  digit/letter counts within a single country**, a new case not seen in
  Iraq/Jordan/Kuwait/Saudi.
- Top color bar, not a side band, indicates category — a third layout shape
  alongside Iraq's side band and Saudi's center strip:
  - Light blue: private
  - Orange: taxi
  - Dark blue: police
  - Red: trucks/tractors
  - Brown: commercial
  - Yellow: unpaid customs
  - Green: diplomatic
- "Egypt" in English + Arabic on the colored top bar.
- **Specific Arabic letters excluded** (ث, خ, ز) to avoid visual confusion —
  a validation-rule detail, comparable to Saudi's "only 17 of 28 letters"
  restriction, worth keeping as a per-country validator note rather than a
  universal rule.
