# Luxembourg — evidence dossier

**First pass, one head-of-state finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Luxembourg).

- 520×110mm, `AB 1234`, black on yellow (default, joins the yellow-default
  outlier list). Separate smaller format for mopeds.
- **`Grand-Ducal` category: single-digit plates reserved exclusively for
  the royal family** — a monarchy-specific category (joins Brunei/Bhutan),
  plus a distinct `Chamber of Deputies` parliamentary series (`P1`-`P99`)
  — two separate high-office categories in one country.
- Custom/vanity plates cost a specific fixed fee (€200) — a pricing detail,
  not design, but notable as the first dossier to give an actual number.
- 2003: current scheme enacted by parliament, replacing older digit-only
  and single-letter formats.
