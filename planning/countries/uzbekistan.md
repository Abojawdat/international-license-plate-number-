# Uzbekistan — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Uzbekistan).

- 520×110mm. Two concurrent serial patterns (`01 A123BC` vs `01 123ABC`) —
  another within-country format variance case.
- Colors: black/white standard, white/green diplomatic, blue/white UN,
  black/yellow foreign citizens, white/green foreign companies — foreign-
  ownership gets its own dedicated categories (distinct from diplomatic),
  not seen with this granularity elsewhere.
- Current system since 2009 (plates since 1996) — a 2-era history.
