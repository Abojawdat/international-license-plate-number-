# Gabon — evidence dossier

**First pass, good category coverage.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Gabon).

- Current (2013-present): 520×110mm, `AB-123-CD`, black on yellow —
  explicitly copies France's 2009 SIV scheme. CEMAC emblem (see
  `republic-of-congo.md`) + "RG" code.
- Previous (pre-2013): `1234G5A`, 9 province codes G1-G9, same yellow/black
  colors — a clean 2-era history where the *colors* stayed constant across
  the format change (unusual; most format changes found also change color).
- Categories: taxi (black/white, distinct from the yellow default), military
  (white/black, numeric only), diplomatic (white/green), temporary NGO
  import (white/red, `ITA` suffix) — a full category table, comparable
  depth to Kuwait's.
