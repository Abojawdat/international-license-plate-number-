# Monaco — evidence dossier

**First pass, richly documented royal-plate case.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Monaco).

- Front 260×110mm, rear 340×110mm — different sizes per face again
  (joins Tunisia/Italy/Switzerland).
- Light-blue/white standard. Provisional: white/red. Diplomatic: blue
  (`CC`)/green (`CD`). International agencies (IAEA, IHO — Monaco hosts
  international bodies): white/green. Tourist: red/white, `TT` prefix.
- **Royal Family: distinct coat of arms plus a `000`-prefixed plate
  reserved for Prince Albert II's family**, with a specific documented
  example (Prince Rainier III's custom "AN 1997" plate) — the most
  concretely-documented head-of-state plate case found (vs. Brunei/
  Bhutan/Luxembourg/Guyana/Barbados/Aruba's more general mentions).
- Rear plates show a tax-validation number and "Principauté de Monaco"
  text — a fixed jurisdiction name alongside the numeric validation.
