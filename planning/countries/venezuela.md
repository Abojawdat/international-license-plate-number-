# Venezuela — evidence dossier

**Second pass, category gap partly closed.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Venezuela), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/venezuela.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/venezuela.html), plus INTT (Venezuela's own national land-transport institute — a rare primary-source government site) and taxi-specific coverage found on a targeted search.

- Current (since 2008): 300×150mm, `AB123CD` (7 characters). Black relief
  characters on a background showing the full national tricolor flag —
  the whole field carries the flag, not just a strip/band, a more
  saturated version of Venezuela's own predecessor design.
- State name shown at bottom, "Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela" at top —
  full official country name spelled out, more verbose than most dossiers'
  country codes.
- **Taxis: white or black only** — white for taxi-line-affiliated vehicles
  registered before INTT, black specifically for taxis affiliated with
  5-star hotels/ports/airports — a status-tier distinction *within* the
  taxi category itself, not seen elsewhere. Mototaxis: yellow.
- Previous system (1982-1997): 3 letters + 3 digits, with color-coding for
  school buses, taxis, public transport and government agencies — a
  category-color scheme that existed in the *legacy* format and was
  apparently simplified away in the 2008 redesign (worth comparing against
  Spain's/Ethiopia's own drop-the-region/category-detail direction).
