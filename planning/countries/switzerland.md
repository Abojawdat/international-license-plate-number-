# Switzerland — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Switzerland).

- **Front (300×80mm) and rear (300×160mm or 500×110mm) are different
  dimensions and different aspect ratios** — not just different sizes like
  Tunisia/Italy, genuinely different shapes per face; the rear even has
  two legal options itself.
- 26 cantons, each a 2-letter code; **rear plate carries both the national
  and cantonal coat of arms** — two emblems on one plate, more than any
  other dossier's single-emblem cases.
- Colors: white standard, green agricultural, yellow utility/construction,
  red bicycle-carrier plates (a vehicle-accessory getting its own plate
  category, not seen elsewhere), diplomatic prefixes CD/CC/AT.
