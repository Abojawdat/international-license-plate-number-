# Spain — evidence dossier

**First pass.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Spain), [matriculasdelmundo.com/en/spain.html](https://matriculasdelmundo.com/en/spain.html).

- Current since Sept 2000: `1234 ABC`, 520×110mm, black on white, EU
  euroband + "E".
- **Modern plates deliberately show no province at all** — two older
  systems (from 1900) were province-coded, but the current one dropped
  that entirely. A cleaner, more deliberate version of Sri Lanka's/
  Ethiopia's move away from regional codes — worth treating as a real,
  recurring design direction (simplify away from regional codes over time),
  not a one-off.
- Taxi/private-hire: white on blue. Large trailers: black on red.
