# Uruguay — evidence dossier

**First pass, confirms Mercosur finding a 3rd time.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Uruguay).

- Since 2016: the same Mercosur `PlateBlank` as Brazil/Argentina (400×130,
  blue top strip, FE-Schrift) — 3rd country confirmed sharing the literal
  blank, strengthening `architecture-notes.md` finding #21/#11.
- Department letter prefix (S=Montevideo, B=Maldonado, A=Canelones).
- Pre-2001: **each department had its own unique plate design** — Uruguay
  itself was federated-by-design before unifying, the same shape of
  transition Ethiopia/South Africa are currently undergoing, but already
  completed here.
- **Maldonado uniquely delayed Mercosur adoption until Jan 2019**, years
  after the rest of the country switched in 2016 — an internal rollout-lag
  case, similar in kind to Kuwait's and Brazil's own phased rollouts, but
  here it's one department lagging the rest of an already-unified country
  rather than a nationwide staged rollout.
