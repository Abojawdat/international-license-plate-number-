# Dominican Republic — evidence dossier

**First pass, deep format history.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_the_Dominican_Republic).

- 152×300mm since 1967 (standardized to NA size that year). Current format
  `AB 01234`; `L`-prefix pickups, `G`-prefix SUVs (vehicle-body-type
  encoded in the prefix, not just category).
- **1930-1989: semi-annual registration** — new plates issued twice yearly
  with alternating colors specifically to show whether registration was
  current, a validity-signaling mechanism through color-alternation over
  time, unlike any other dossier's history.
- At least 5 distinct color/format eras from 1930 to 2017 (including a
  2017 "hybrid" plate with green top/bottom stripes) — one of the deepest
  format histories found, alongside Kuwait/Greece/DRC.
- 2018 temporary plates: laminated paper pouches, not aluminum — a
  material distinct from every other dossier's assumed metal/plastic.
