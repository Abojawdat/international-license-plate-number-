# Serbia — evidence dossier

**First pass, one novel exclusion finding.** [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Serbia).

- 520.5×112.9mm. Format `BG 123-AB`, blue "SRB" strip.
- Rich category table: agricultural (green), moped (yellow), motorcycle
  (white), historic (`OT`, white), taxi (`TX`), trailer (reversed
  letter/number order — a structural, not just color, category
  distinction), police (blue, Cyrillic `П`), military (single letter +
  Armed Forces emblem), diplomatic (yellow/black or black/yellow).
- **Since 2017: special Cyrillic letters (Ć, Č, Š, Ž, Đ) plus Y and W
  stopped being issued on new plates** — a 5th distinct exclusion-reason
  category: simplifying toward broader international readability, related
  to but distinct from Greece/Ukraine/Cyprus's cross-script-compatibility
  reason (this is dropping characters *entirely* going forward, not
  picking a compatible subset from the start).
- Current system since Jan 1, 2011, old and new plates coexisted through
  the transition year.
